Questions

When will my order ship?

We print and ship every Saturday. Orders placed by Friday go out the next Saturday. For Christmas, order by Dec 11. Ornaments ship within the US for now. Pup Club works anywhere.

Is the ornament safe for my dog?

It’s made for your tree, not for chewing. Hang it high, and don’t let dogs play with it or eat it.

Can I cancel Pup Club?

Yes, any time. Use the manage link in your receipt email and you won’t be billed again.

Are the recipes safe for every dog?

They use ingredients commonly considered dog-safe. If your dog has allergies or a special diet, check with your vet first.

Shipping and returns

Shipping

Ornaments ship every Saturday within the US for a flat $4.99, with tracking.

Returns

Personalized ornaments are made to order, so we can’t take them back. If one arrives damaged or the name is wrong because of our mistake, we’ll reprint it or refund you. Email us within 7 days with a photo.

Pup Club

Cancel any time. You keep access through the month you paid for, and we don’t refund partial months.
